## Why AfLux?

Every financial tool today wants your data in their cloud. Mint did it. Rocket Money does it. Even your bank's "budgeting" feature uploads everything to their servers.

AfLux takes a different approach: **everything stays on your machine.**

## What It Does

AfLux combines the features of Mint (cash flow tracking, subscription detection), TurboTax (tax document ingestion, return comparison), and a financial advisor (AI-powered analysis) — all running locally on your Mac or Windows PC.

- **Import bank statements** (OFX/QFX/CSV) — no screen scraping, no bank credentials shared
- **Track rental properties** with full Schedule E expense categorization
- **Plan retirement** with Monte Carlo simulations and Roth conversion analysis
- **Ask Lux** — chat with a local AI grounded in your actual financial data

## How Privacy Works

Your data is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256) using a passphrase only you know. The AI runs locally via Ollama — no API keys, no cloud calls. We literally cannot see your data because it never leaves your device.
