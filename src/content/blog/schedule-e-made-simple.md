## The Schedule E Problem

If you own rental property, your CPA needs a clean breakdown of expenses by IRS category every tax season: management fees (Line 11), mortgage interest (Line 12), repairs (Line 14), property taxes (Line 16), depreciation (Line 18), and a dozen other line items.

Most landlords track this in a spreadsheet. Some use Quicken. A few brave souls use Stessa or Baselane — cloud-based tools that store your rental financials on their servers.

The spreadsheet is tedious. The cloud tools mean your property addresses, tenant names, rent amounts, and expense history live on someone else's infrastructure.
